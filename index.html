About OUCB

Oxford’s society for chemistry and biochemistry

We run regular academic talks, workshops and social events for students with a passion for chemistry, biochemistry and other STEM subjects.

OUCB is a chance to meet people across colleges and years, and to talk to professionals working in different fields of science. We often collaborate with other Oxford science societies.
